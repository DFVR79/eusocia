Damien Fèvre, PhD
Founder · Scientist · Consultant

EUSOCIA was founded by Damien Fèvre, a geneticist and bioinformatician with international experience in academic research, applied genetics and biological data science.

His work spans molecular biology, genomics, population genetics and quantitative approaches, with particular expertise in honeybee biology and breeding.

This scientific background is complemented by practical experience in beekeeping, agroecology, biodiversity and higher education, allowing EUSOCIA to connect data analysis with the biological systems from which those data originate.
